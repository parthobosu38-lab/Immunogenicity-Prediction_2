HOW TO READ: every score is oriented so that higher = more immunogenic; nothing is flipped after the fact. 'ens_minus_score' is the ensemble AUC minus the score's AUC, with a paired bootstrap CI; if the CI contains 0 the difference is not demonstrated.
CAVEATS to state in the paper: (1) the rank is the ensemble's own main input; (2) PRIME and the IEDB predictor were trained on IEDB-derived data and may have seen external peptides; (3) PRIME and IEDB are class I only; (4) 'class' is the predicted best class, not an experimental restriction; (5) the tools predict binding / CD8 immunogenicity, the labels are T-cell assay results.
A score with AUC < 0.5 in a subset means its usual direction is inverted there (e.g. MHC rank in class I).
